## Scope

`src/pages/index.astro`, the only page. Mode: Persuade. Mobile-first: visitors arrive from a QR on a printed card, seconds after meeting Ana at a sale or a fair stand.

## Strategy

- Primary action: open the online store, anavende.com.ar. Secondary: WhatsApp catalog, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp chat.
- Payments and delivery area live on anavende.com.ar; this page does not restate them.
- Memorable moment: the stand's pegboard, with every channel hanging from a hook and swinging when touched.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the fair stand itself. A perforated board hangs every channel as a product card on a metal hook. It refuses the vertical column of identical pill buttons.

OWN-WORLD: The brand bordó #822733 drenches the whole surface as a pegboard, with holes punched in a deep bordó #4a1219. Cards are printed stock in white and bordó ink. One sticker yellow #f2c230 marks the store card. Euro-slot hang holes, steel hooks, real hanging shadows. Icons sit in bordó square tiles that echo the logo's 2×2 grid. Archivo is used across widths, expanded and heavy for the sign, normal for captions.

STORY: The visitor recognizes the stand they just saw. They see that Ana sells tech accessories. Then they tap the big yellow store card.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At 390×844, the stand sign sits at the top: logo tile, ANA VENDE and the typed "Vos elegís…" line. Below it, the full-width yellow store card hangs from two hooks, with its action label visible. Then a 2×2 cluster of smaller hang-tags at varied heights and tilts, with the top of the cluster visible without scrolling.

FORM: Tablero de feria. Position 1 on the ordered list, taken as IMPECCABLE'S PICK. Seed key b4e3cb8c.

Signature interaction: cards drop onto their hooks with a damped pendulum settle and swing from the hook when touched or hovered. Scrolling gives them a slight sway. With reduced motion everything is static.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
